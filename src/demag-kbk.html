Demag KBK

Modular light cranes for ergonomic handling.

KBK is Demag's modular crane construction kit: profiles, trolleys and suspensions that build into workstation cranes, monorails and jibs. Demag rates KBK for loads from 80 kg to 3.2 t.

It suits assembly lines and stations where an operator moves the same parts all day and the system may grow or change with the line.
